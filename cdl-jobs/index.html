Before You Apply: Official Driver Resources

Road Ready Crew is based in Florida, and the city pages above cover hubs around the country. Before you apply with a carrier, these official sites are the ones that matter for your license, medical card and Clearinghouse record. We don't run those systems, and we never ask for your Social Security number, date of birth or license number.

For Florida drivers

  • FLHSMV Commercial Driver License page — CDL classes, knowledge and skills tests, endorsements, and transferring an out-of-state CDL.
  • Official Florida CDL Handbook (PDF) — the study source Florida publishes for the CDL knowledge and skills tests, including the pre-trip inspection.
  • FLHSMV CDL medical certification — how Florida expects you to keep a current medical examiner's certificate on file with the state.

For every CDL driver

  • FMCSA Drug and Alcohol Clearinghouse — register so you can give electronic consent when a carrier runs a full query. Our Clearinghouse guide explains how that works.
  • National Registry of Certified Medical Examiners — search by ZIP code for an examiner who can issue a DOT medical card. See our DOT medical card basics guide.
  • Our CDL-A pre-trip inspection basics guide covers the federal daily inspection and DVIR rules in plain English, with links to the official eCFR text.
