CDL-A Truck Driver Jobs in Savannah, GA

Savannah is home to one of the busiest container ports in the country. I-95 runs along the coast, and I-16 heads straight inland to Macon and Atlanta, carrying a steady stream of containers.

Main highways: I-95, I-16 and I-516.

What Freight Moves Through Savannah

The Port of Savannah is one of the largest container ports on the East Coast, which drives a lot of port drayage and container hauling. Distribution centers have gone up all around the port and along I-16 and I-95 to handle imports. Freight includes retail goods, consumer products, auto and anything coming off the ships.

Types of Runs From the Savannah Area

Local drivers often run port drayage, pulling containers to nearby warehouses. Regional lanes run up I-16 to Atlanta, along I-95 to Jacksonville and the Carolinas, and across South Georgia. OTR drivers haul imports inland to the Southeast and Midwest.

Every carrier is different, so pay, home time and equipment depend on the company and the account. Get the details in writing before you sign on.
