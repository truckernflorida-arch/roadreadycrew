FAQ

Straight answers.

Do drivers pay anything?

No. Not to sign up, not to get placed, not out of your first check. Ever. If anybody claiming to be us asks you for money, it isn't us.

So how do you get paid?

The trucking company pays us a fee when they hire a driver we sent and that driver gets dispatched. Your pay doesn't change because you came through us.

What do I need to qualify?

Most of our jobs need a CDL-A from a U.S. state, the right to work in the U.S., solid English, and a clean-enough record for that carrier. Every carrier sets its own minimums for experience, accidents and violations. We'll tell you up front which ones you fit.

Do you need my SSN or license number?

Not from us. We never ask for your Social Security number, date of birth or license number. You give those only to the carrier, on the carrier's own official application.

Who sees my info?

We do. We only pass your profile to a carrier after we've talked with you about that job. We don't sell your info. See our privacy & SMS policy.

Lease-purchase or owner-operator?

We focus on company-driver seats. If a lease-purchase or lease-on deal ever comes up, we'll tell you plainly what you're signing up for, including the parts the ad leaves out.

How fast can I get seated?

It depends on the carrier's background check, drug test and orientation dates. We'll give you an honest timeline for each job instead of a sales line.

Ready to roll?

Two minutes on the form. One straight-talk phone call. Then let's get you seated.
