What we screen for

We filter before you spend a minute.

  • CDL class and the endorsements your freight needs
  • Verified experience against your minimums
  • Self-reported accidents and moving violations for the last 3 years
  • Freight, route type and home-time fit, so they don't quit week two
  • English proficiency and U.S.-domiciled CDL / work authorization
  • Hiring area: drivers who live where you can hire
  • A real phone conversation about your actual pay, miles and equipment

What you keep control of

Your process. Your call.

We're a referral source, not a staffing company. Drivers apply on your system and you make every hiring decision.

  • Your own DOT employment application (Tenstreet, DriverReach or your own link, tagged with our source code)
  • MVR and PSP pulls
  • FMCSA Drug & Alcohol Clearinghouse queries
  • Pre-employment drug test and road test
  • Orientation, offer and final hiring decision

We never collect SSNs, dates of birth or license numbers, and we don't run background checks.
